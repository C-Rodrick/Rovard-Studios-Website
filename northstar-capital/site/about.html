Northstar/About

We built Northstar so no business owner has to guess where they stand.

Small businesses employ nearly half of America’s workforce, and most of them still run on spreadsheets, instinct and a bank app. We think they deserve better software.

Why “Northstar”

A fixed point to steer by.

Navigators have crossed oceans by finding one steady point in the sky. A business is no different: the weather changes, the waters get rough, and what you need is something that doesn’t move.

That’s the job of our product — and of our brand. The mark is an N with a wedge pointing north: a small reminder that every decision gets easier once you know where you stand.

Our story

Five years, one idea.

2021

A spreadsheet and a frustration

Elena, a restaurant manager turned operator, builds a cash-flow sheet for her family’s business — and can’t stop other owners asking for a copy.

2022

The first 40 businesses

Jonas joins. A private beta turns the sheet into a product. Forty owners help shape the first invoice and forecast screens.

2023

Forecasting ships

Scenario planning launches. The first customer avoids a payroll shortfall by moving a purchase one week.

2024

5,000 businesses

Invoices become payable online. Northstar passes a thousand five-star reviews from owners who never wanted to be accountants.

2026
