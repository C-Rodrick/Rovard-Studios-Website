The mark: an N that points north

A bold geometric N for stability, with a blue wedge inlaid in its counter — a compass needle, a sail, an arrow.

Direction, built into the letter.

  • Stem = 9 units. Every other measurement is a multiple, so the mark scales cleanly from a favicon to a billboard.
  • The wedge is inlaid, not stuck on. A constant 2.2-unit gap separates it from the ink, like a precision-cut insert.
  • It reads as a chart. Trace the N: up, down, up — a cash-flow line that ends higher than it started.
