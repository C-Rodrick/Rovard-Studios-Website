Cookie Preferences

Short version: this website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, so there is nothing for you to switch off.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can save in your browser. They are often used to remember you, count visitors or show ads.

What this website does

  • It does not set advertising or tracking cookies
  • It does not use analytics tools that follow you around the web
  • It does not need a cookie to work, so there is no banner to accept

Our hosting provider and our contact form provider (Formspree) may process technical information such as your IP address when you load the site or send a message. That is described in our Privacy Policy.

Content from other sites

If we link to or embed something from another site (for example a video or a social profile), that site may set its own cookies when you use it. We do not control those, so please check their policies.

Your control

You can always block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If we ever add analytics or other optional cookies, we will ask for your permission first and add the controls to this page.
