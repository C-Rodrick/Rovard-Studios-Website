ROVARD STUDIOS Brand Film

A 90-second brand film that turns the ROVARD STUDIOS identity, services and selected work into one continuous piece of motion.

What we did
Motion Design / Kinetic Typography / Sound Design
Year
2026

The challenge

The studio needed a single film that could introduce who ROVARD STUDIOS is, what it does and the quality of its work in under two minutes.

The creative thinking

We built every frame from the brand system itself: Syne and Space Grotesk kinetic type, the blue and yellow palette, real project work and a soundtrack composed to cut on every bar.
