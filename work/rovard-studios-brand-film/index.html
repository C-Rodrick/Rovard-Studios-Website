Rovard Studios Brand Film

A 90-second brand film that turns the Rovard identity, services and selected work into one continuous piece of motion.

Services: Motion Design / Kinetic Typography / Sound Design

The challenge

The studio needed a single film that could introduce who Rovard is, what it does and the quality of its work in under two minutes.

The approach

We built every frame from the brand system itself: Syne and Space Grotesk kinetic type, the blue and yellow palette, real project work and a soundtrack composed to cut on every bar.

The outcome

A premium, on-brand film ready for the website, social channels and pitch presentations.
