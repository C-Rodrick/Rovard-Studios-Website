Brand Identity Design · 2026

Bitefort

A confident identity and digital direction for a privacy-first cookie management platform.

Services: Art Direction / Identity / Messaging

The challenge

Bitefort needed a distinctive brand system that could make complex privacy and compliance feel clear, credible and approachable.

The approach

We built a dark, high-contrast visual language around the Bitefort shield and cookie motif, extending it across campaigns, digital touchpoints and branded merchandise.

The outcome

A memorable purple identity that makes trust, privacy and effortless cookie management feel immediate across every touchpoint.
