Northstar Capital Ad

A 30-second 3D launch ad for the Northstar Capital website and dashboard, presenting the concept as a real product.

Services: Motion Design / 3D / Product Film / Sound Design

The challenge

Northstar needed an ad that could sell clarity: show a calm, trustworthy fintech product in motion and make a small-business owner want to try it within half a minute.

The approach

We built the N mark as a lit, extruded 3D object and placed the real website and dashboard screens in a virtual 3D space: a floating browser that scrolls the live homepage, data cards lifting off the glass, a dashboard carousel and phones rising in perspective, all cut to an original 120 BPM score.

The outcome

A 30-second, 60 fps product ad ready for social, the website and pitch decks. Self-initiated concept project; Northstar Capital is a fictional brand and all figures are illustrative.
