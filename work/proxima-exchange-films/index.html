Proxima Exchange Films

Two launch films for a crypto and forex trading platform: a cinematic 3D brand film and a precise product film built from the real interface.

Independent concept. A self-initiated project by the studio, not a client commission.

Project type
Independent creative exploration
What we did
Motion Design / 3D / Cinematic Direction / Sound Design
Year
2026

The brief

Proxima Exchange needed to feel trustworthy and premium in a category full of noise, and to show both the emotion of the brand and the real product in motion.

The creative thinking

We built the logo as a layered 3D object and shot it with a virtual camera: rack focus, light rays and lens streaks, a deep blue and gold grade, and a score cut to every hit. A second, frame-accurate film runs the actual website UI, live chart and markets table on the same timeline.

Visual direction

  • #000081
  • #ffffff
  • #1C39BB
  • #FFC000

What was made

Two films that work together: a 40-second cinematic hook for campaigns and social, and a 30-second product film for the website and case studies. Concept project; figures shown are illustrative.
