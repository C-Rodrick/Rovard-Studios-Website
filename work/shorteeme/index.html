ShorteeMe

A bright, human social campaign system helping ShorteeMe make financing feel clear, accessible and full of possibility.

Services: Campaign System / Content Direction

The challenge

ShorteeMe needed a consistent content system that could communicate funding opportunities while keeping people and optimism at the centre.

The approach

We created a flexible campaign language built from bold blue fields, energetic orange accents, expressive headlines and real human stories.

The outcome

A recognizable social presence that makes ShorteeMe’s message easy to understand, share and remember across every campaign format.
