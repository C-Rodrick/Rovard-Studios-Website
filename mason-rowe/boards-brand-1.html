04  /  The monogramSelf-initiated concept project

The Shared Pier.

An M and an R share a single vertical, the way a pier carries two arches. It is the one structural element nobody removes — and the idea the company is built on: a mason and an architect, one drawing board.

M — the mason

Two legs, one V: weight carried down.

R — the architect

A stem, a bowl, a long diagonal: line and curve.

One pier, in bronze

Heavier, warmer: the shared structure.

08  /  Color accessibilitySelf-initiated concept project

Checked,
not assumed.

Every pairing is measured against WCAG 2.1. The pure brand bronze reaches 4.2:1 on ivory — fine for large display type and graphics, not for small text — so two companions exist: Bronze ink for small text on light, and Bronze light on dark.
