11  /  Art direction · photographySelf-initiated concept project

Blue hour,
true verticals.

  1. 01Shoot exteriors at dusk, with the building lit from within.
  2. 02Keep verticals parallel: shift-lens, never converging.
  3. 03Show material in raking light: stone, bronze, oak, travertine.
  4. 04People appear as scale, never as the subject.
  5. 05Warm highlights, cool shadows, muted greens, fine grain.
  6. 06No stock palm-and-champagne clichés. No lens flare.

All imagery on this board is a fictional concept render, art-directed to the same grade a photographer would deliver.
