About

A mason and an architect.

Mason & Rowe began in 2011 with a shared conviction: that a home is a long-term structure, and should be built like one.

Our story

Two names, one pier.

Eleanor Mason grew up setting stone in her family’s yard. Julian Rowe trained as an architect and spent a decade drawing buildings he would never see finished.

They met on a restoration site, arguing about a limestone cornice. The argument produced a company: part workshop, part studio, and entirely unwilling to separate the way a building is drawn from the way it is made.

Our monogram is built on that idea. An M and an R share a single vertical, the way a pier carries two arches. It is the one structural element nobody removes.
