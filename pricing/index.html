Questions

How do I get started?

Tell us what you're working on. Use the form or message us. A few words is plenty. We'll ask a few questions, then send a written quote.

Do you take small jobs, like one flyer?

Yes. One flyer, one logo or one card is a real project here. Small jobs are how we earn trust.

How much will it cost?

It depends on what you need and how big it is. You get a written quote before any work starts, so there are no surprises. Tell us your budget range and we'll tell you honestly what it can cover.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the project and how fast we get your feedback. We put the timeline in your quote so you know what to expect.

How many changes can I ask for?

Two rounds of changes are included with each piece of work. More than that, or a change of direction after you approve a stage, is quoted separately. It keeps the project focused.

What do I receive at the end?

The final approved files, ready for print or screen. Need the original working files too? We agree that in the quote.

Do you also print things?

We design the artwork and prepare print-ready files. A printer or installer handles printing, signage and vehicle wraps, and we're happy to point you to a good one.

Who owns the design?

You do, once the project is paid in full. The details are in our Terms.

Can you work with people outside Canada?

Yes. We're based in Canada and work with people all over the world.

I don't know what I need. Can you still help?

That's a normal place to start. Tell us your idea in your own words, or choose "I'm not sure" on the form. We'll figure out the rest together.

Ask for a quote.

A few words about your idea is plenty. We reply with a written quote before any work starts.
