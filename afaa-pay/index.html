The problem

Online trade runs on "trust me."

Informal commerce moves billions across WhatsApp, Facebook and phone calls. Neither side can verify the other, and when something goes wrong there is nothing to fall back on.

01

Pay first, hope later

Buyers send money to people they have never met and wait for goods that may never arrive.

02

Deliver first, chase payment

Freelancers and suppliers finish the work, then spend weeks chasing an invoice.

03

No record, no recourse

Voice notes are not contracts. Disputes become arguments with no evidence and no referee.

AI trust score

A reputation that travels with you.

Every completed deal, on-time delivery and clean dispute record feeds a score from 300 to 850. Higher scores unlock faster payouts and lower fees. No bank history required.

  • Behaviour, not paperwork. Built from real transaction signals, so first-time merchants can earn credibility fast.
  • Fraud signals in real time. Pattern models flag mismatched evidence and risky counterparties before money moves.
  • Explainable. Every point has a reason you can see and improve.
