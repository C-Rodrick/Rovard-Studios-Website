About & security

Your money is never on the line.

We're building the neutral ground where Africa's buyers and sellers can meet, agree and be paid, with a referee that never takes sides.

Security

Defence in depth, by default.

Ring-fenced funds

Escrow balances sit in segregated accounts at licensed partner banks, never mixed with operating funds.

End-to-end encryption

AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. Contract documents are hashed and time-stamped.

Verified identities

Phone, national ID and liveness checks before a merchant can receive funds.

Real-time fraud AI

Every transaction is scored in under 200ms for velocity, device and evidence anomalies.

Compliance

Built to regulators' standards.

The concept follows the frameworks a licensed payment institution would operate under.

PCI-DSS alignedCOBAC / BEAC e-money rulesKYC & AML screeningISO 27001 practicesGDPR-style data rights
Our story

From a market stall to a platform.

A concept shaped by one question: what if every informal deal had a referee?

The insight

Trust is the real currency

The starting hypothesis: the biggest barrier to online trade isn't payment access. It's not trusting the other party.

The product

Escrow meets mobile money

We paired bank-grade escrow with the rails people already use every day: MTN MoMo and Orange Money.

The system

An AI trust score

Reputation that compounds with every honest deal, giving the unbanked a financial identity.

The goal

Pan-African by design

One contract layer, many currencies, from Cameroon to Nigeria, Ghana and Kenya.
