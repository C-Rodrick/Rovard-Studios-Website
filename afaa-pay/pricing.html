FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who pays the escrow fee?

You choose: the buyer, the seller or split 50/50. The fee is shown on the contract before anyone signs.

What happens if there's a dispute?

Funds stay frozen. Both sides upload evidence and a mediator decides within 72 hours. Funds are released or refunded accordingly.

Can I pay with mobile money?

Yes. MTN MoMo and Orange Money are first-class payment rails, alongside cards and bank transfers.

How does my trust score lower my fees?

Merchants with a score above 680 qualify for faster payouts, and above 760 for instant release and reduced rates on Business and Platform.
