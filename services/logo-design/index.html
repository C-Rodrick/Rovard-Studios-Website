Questions

Good to know

Not here? Ask us.

What's the difference between a logo and a brand identity?

A logo is the mark that identifies you. A brand identity adds the colours, lettering and rules for using them, so everything you make looks consistent.

Can you refresh my existing logo?

Yes. We can sharpen what you have or start over, whichever fits your goals.

What do you need from me to start?

Your name, a few lines about what you do and who it's for, and any logos or colours you like or dislike. A few words is plenty.
