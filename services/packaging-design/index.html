Good to know

Do you make the packaging?

We design the artwork and prepare print-ready files. The packaging itself is made by a printer or manufacturer.

What do you need from me?

The packaging type and dimensions (or your printer's template), your logo, and any text that must appear.

Can you design a book cover?

Yes. Book covers are one of the things we design.
