Packaging Design for Products & Publications

We design packaging and printed pieces that make products feel considered, desirable and unmistakably yours on the shelf and in the hand.

Who it is for

Product businesses launching or refreshing a range, food and consumer brands that need to stand out on the shelf, and authors and publishers who need a cover that sells the book.

How we approach it

We look at where the product is sold, what sits next to it and what the buyer needs to understand at a glance. The design is then built to work in the real format, with artwork prepared to your printer’s specifications.

What's included

  • Packaging concept and direction
  • Label and box design
  • Range and variant system
  • Book cover design
  • Mockups for presentation and e-commerce
  • Print-ready artwork files

Our process

Discover the real problem, define the direction, design the solution, refine it until it holds together, and deliver organized, production-ready files with the guidance to use them.

Frequently asked questions

Do you print the packaging?

We design it and prepare print-ready files. Production is handled by your printer or manufacturer, and we can work with their specifications.

Can you design a whole product range?

Yes. We create a system so each product variant is distinct while the range still reads as one brand.
