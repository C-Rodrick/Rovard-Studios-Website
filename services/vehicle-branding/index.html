Capabilities / Vehicle branding

Your vehicle is already moving around the city. Make it work for you.

A branded vehicle is advertising that goes to your customers. We design wraps for cars, vans and delivery vehicles that people notice and remember.

Who it's for

  • Companies with a fleet
  • Anyone who wants their car or van to carry their brand

What you get

  • Print-ready files for your installer
  • Help finding the right wrap installer

What people ask us for

  • Full vehicle wraps
  • Cars
  • Vans
  • Delivery vehicles
  • Company and commercial vehicles

We design it and prepare print-ready files. Printing and installation are done by a printer or installer, and we're happy to point you in the right direction.

Good to know

Do you install the wrap?

We design the artwork and prepare the files. Installation is done by a wrap installer, and we can point you to one.

What do you need from me?

The make and model of the vehicle (or a template from your installer), your logo, and the message and contact details to show.

Can one design work for several vehicles?

Yes. We can adapt one design across a fleet.
