Brand Identity Design Studio in Canada

A brand identity is more than a logo. We design complete identity systems that make clear what your business stands for and keep it recognizable everywhere it appears.

Who it is for

Businesses launching something new, companies whose look no longer matches where they are going, and teams that need one consistent system instead of a collection of disconnected files.

How we approach it

Every identity starts with positioning: who you serve, what you want them to believe and what makes you different. The visual decisions follow from that, so the mark, palette and type all carry the same idea instead of decorating it.

What's included

  • Brand strategy, positioning and personality
  • Tone of voice and key messaging
  • Logo, symbol and lockup suite
  • Colour palette with accessible contrast pairings
  • Typography system
  • Graphic language, iconography and photography direction
  • Brand guidelines document
  • Applications such as stationery, signage, packaging and social templates

Our process

Discover the real problem, define the direction, design the solution, refine it until it holds together, and deliver organized, production-ready files with the guidance to use them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a logo and a brand identity?

A logo is one mark. A brand identity is the full system around it: colours, typography, graphic elements, imagery, voice and the rules for using them together, so the brand looks and sounds consistent everywhere.

Do you work with businesses outside Canada?

Yes. Rovard Studios is based in Canada and works with clients worldwide. Projects run remotely, with clear check-ins at each stage.

What do I receive at the end?

Organized, production-ready files for print and digital, plus brand guidelines that explain how to use everything so your team can apply the identity with confidence.

How much does a brand identity cost?

It depends on scope. Use the project form to share your goals, budget range and timeline, and we will come back with a recommendation that fits.
