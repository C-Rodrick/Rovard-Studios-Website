What every flyer needs before it goes out

October 6, 2026 · 3 min read · by ROVARD STUDIOS

A flyer has one job: get the right person to do one thing. Most flyers that don't work are missing something simple.

The checklist

  • What it is, in a few words, large enough to read from a distance
  • When and where, written out in full
  • One clear thing you want people to do (call, scan, come, buy)
  • Contact details that actually work: check every number and spelling
  • Your logo, so people remember who sent it

Less is louder

The temptation is to fit everything on. Resist it. One strong headline, a short supporting line and the details will be read. Ten things competing for attention won't.

Check how it will be seen

A flyer on a phone screen needs bigger text than one on a wall. If it will be printed, ask for the right size and a bleed (extra space at the edge) so nothing is cut off. If you're not sure, tell us where it's going and we'll set it up properly.

Proofread out loud

Read every word aloud, then ask someone else to do the same. Dates and phone numbers are where mistakes hide.
