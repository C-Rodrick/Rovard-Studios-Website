One studio.
Multiple creative dimensions.

  1. 01

    Brand Worlds

    Brand strategy, identity systems, logo design, typography and visual language.

    A complete way of looking and sounding, written down so anyone can keep using it. For new businesses, people building a personal brand, and established companies that want to look the part again.

    See LinkRithm, Green Blueprint, Vita House

  2. 02

    Digital Experiences

    Website design, digital interfaces, interactive experiences and digital environments.

    Websites and interfaces that people understand quickly, built to work well on a phone. For businesses, organizations and individuals who need to explain what they do online.

    See Mason & Rowe Residences, Northstar Capital Website, Afa'a Pay

  3. 03

    Motion & Visual Stories

    Motion graphics, animated identities, promotional videos and visual storytelling.

    Moving work that explains an idea quickly and stays with people. For launches, campaigns and anyone with a story that reads better in motion.

    See ROVARD STUDIOS Brand Film, Northstar Capital Ad, Proxima Exchange Films

  4. 04

    Creative Communication

    Graphic design, campaigns, promotional materials and advertising visuals.

    Flyers, posters, social media, billboards, banners, packaging and vehicle graphics that say one thing clearly. For events, churches and ministries, small businesses and campaigns. One flyer is a real project here.

    See ShorteeMe, JoJo Foods, PDOCA

  5. 05

    Creative Direction

    Concept development, art direction, visual strategy and creative problem-solving.

    The thinking that holds everything together, and a clear direction before anything gets made. For founders and teams with many moving parts, or a good idea that hasn't found its shape yet.

    See Mason & Rowe, Northstar Capital, Vita House

    Discover

  3. 03

    Design

    You see real work, not a pitch about work.

  4. 04

    Your feedback sharpens it. We refine it and build it out to everything it needs to become.

ROVARD STUDIOS is a multidisciplinary creative studio, based in Canada and working with people around the world.

We work with individuals, small businesses, churches, organizations and companies. Some projects are big and some are one flyer. Same care, same questions: who is this for, and what should it do?

About the studio
Vision
To create meaningful visual experiences that expand the possibilities of communication, creativity and design.
Mission
To help individuals, brands and organisations express their ideas through thoughtful design, creative technology and exceptional visual storytelling.

Have an idea? Let's make it matter.

Whether you're starting something new, building a brand or looking for a fresh creative perspective, let's explore what we can create together.
