Questions

Good things to know.

Do I need a membership to book a visit?
No. Open Door is free and lets you book any service. Insurance is accepted at every Vita House location.
Does membership replace insurance?
No. Membership covers access and certain included services. For care beyond those, insurance or self-pay rates apply, and we'll explain costs before your visit.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Monthly memberships can be paused or cancelled from your dashboard at any time, with no cancellation fee.
Who counts as a household member on Whole House?
Up to four people living at the same address, including children and partners. Add or remove members from your dashboard.
Is this a real service?
No. Vita House is a fictional brand created by Rovard Studios as a self-initiated design concept. Prices and benefits are illustrative.
