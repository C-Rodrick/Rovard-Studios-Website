FAQs

Quick answers.

How quickly will I hear back?
Messages from this form are answered within one business day. Existing patients get the fastest replies through the secure dashboard.
What insurance do you accept?
Vita House accepts most major plans at every location. Add your insurance details during booking and we'll confirm coverage before your visit.
How do I cancel or reschedule?
Open your dashboard, choose the appointment and select Reschedule or Cancel. Changes are free up to 24 hours before your visit.
Can I bring someone with me?
Of course. A friend or family member is welcome at in-person visits, and can join by video for virtual visits.
Is Vita House a real clinic?
No. It's a fictional concept created by Rovard Studios. No messages sent from this site are delivered anywhere.
