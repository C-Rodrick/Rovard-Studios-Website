About Vita House

A healthcare company, built like a home.

Vita comes from the Latin for “life”. House is where life happens. We combine primary care, prevention and wellness in spaces and software that feel warm, clear and unhurried.

Our idea

Healthcare shouldn't feel like a waiting room.

Too many people put off care because the experience around it feels cold, confusing or rushed. Phone trees, paper forms and portals that read like legal documents.

Vita House starts from a different place: what would it feel like to walk through a door where someone is glad you came? Everything follows from that, from longer appointments and plain-language summaries to a booking flow that takes minutes.

What we believe

Six values, one way of caring.

Warm

We greet people like we're glad to see them, because we are.

Intelligent

Evidence-minded clinicians who explain the “why”, not just the “what”.

Human

Real people answer your messages. Real time is set aside for your questions.

Calm

Quiet spaces, gentle language and interfaces that never shout.

Professional

Rigour behind the kindness: privacy, safety and clear standards.

Contemporary

Booking, messages and records that work as well on a phone as they do in the house.

Accessibility

Designed so everyone can walk in.

Accessibility is a starting point at Vita House, not an afterthought. It shapes our buildings, our website and our dashboard.

  • Readable by default

    17px body text, generous spacing and WCAG AA contrast on every colour pairing.

  • Your preferences, remembered

    Adjust text size and reduce motion in your dashboard settings.

  • Keyboard and screen reader friendly

    Clear focus states, labelled forms and error messages that explain how to fix things.
